Privacy Policy

Welcome to Deen Al Islam. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Deen Al Islam mobile application.

Deen Al Islam is developed and maintained by AROET.

Your privacy matters.

We aim to collect and use only the information necessary to provide the application's features, improve reliability, and support advertising where applicable.

1. Information We May Process

Location Information

Deen Al Islam may request access to your approximate or precise device location to provide location-dependent features such as:

  • Accurate prayer times
  • Qibla direction
  • Nearby mosque search
  • Location-based Islamic utilities

Location access is requested only when required by a feature. You can disable location permission through your Android device settings.

Device & App Information

Certain technical information may be processed automatically by third-party services used by the app, such as device type, operating system information, application interactions, diagnostics, and advertising-related identifiers.

2. How Location Information Is Used

Location information is used to calculate or provide location-dependent services.

Depending on the feature, location-related requests may be sent securely to third-party services used for prayer calculations, reverse geocoding, or map-based search.

The application does not continuously track your location in the background for advertising purposes.

3. Prayer Times & Offline Functionality

Deen Al Islam is designed with an offline-first approach for several Islamic features.

Prayer times can be calculated locally using your saved location coordinates when network connectivity is unavailable.

This allows important prayer functionality to continue working even when your device is temporarily offline.

4. Quran & Hadith Content

Quran and Hadith content included in the application is stored locally within the application resources.

These features are designed to work without requiring continuous internet access.

The application does not collect your Quran reading content or Hadith reading activity for advertising purposes.

5. Zakat & Financial Information

Information entered into the Zakat calculator is used to perform calculations within the application.

Zakat calculation history is stored locally on your device to provide features such as calculation history and PDF export.

Important:

Financial information entered into the Zakat calculator is not intentionally collected by AROET as a personal financial profile.

6. Advertising

Deen Al Islam may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and interaction data according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Where required by applicable law, the application may request consent before serving personalized advertisements.

7. Analytics

We may use analytics services such as Firebase Analytics to understand general application usage and improve stability and user experience.

Analytics may include information such as screen views, application events, device information, and diagnostic information.

We do not intentionally send precise location coordinates or Zakat financial records to analytics services.

8. Notifications

Deen Al Islam may send notifications for features such as prayer reminders and general application updates.

You can enable or disable notifications through the application settings or your Android device settings.

9. Third-Party Services

Some application features may rely on trusted third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase services
  • Prayer calculation services
  • OpenStreetMap / geocoding services
  • Google Maps

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

Network communications used by supported services are transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections where supported.

However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Deen Al Islam is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating personal profiles.

12. Your Choices

You can control several permissions and features directly from your Android device, including:

  • Location permission
  • Notification permission
  • Exact alarm permission
  • Advertising preferences where supported

You may also uninstall the application at any time.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, regulations, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
