1. Introduction

Deen Al Islam ("Deen Al Islam", "we", "us", "our", or the "App") is an Islamic utility application developed by AROET.

The App provides Islamic tools and resources, including prayer times, Quran, Hadith, Qibla direction, Tasbih, Duas, Islamic names, Zakat calculation, mosque discovery and notification features.

We respect your privacy and aim to process only information that is reasonably necessary to provide and improve the App.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the App or its third-party service providers may process different categories of information.

📍 Location

Approximate or precise location may be used for prayer times, Qibla and mosque discovery.

🔔 Notifications

Notification preferences may be stored to provide prayer reminders and updates.

💰 Zakat Data

Zakat calculation records may be stored locally on your device.

📱 Technical Data

Third-party services may process technical and usage information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed when necessary to:

  • Provide accurate local prayer times.
  • Calculate Qibla direction.
  • Find nearby mosques.
  • Provide Quran, Hadith and Islamic resources.
  • Provide Zakat calculations and history.
  • Deliver prayer and general notifications.
  • Display advertisements where applicable.
  • Understand application usage and improve the App.
  • Maintain application security, stability and reliability.

4. Location Services

Deen Al Islam may request approximate or precise location permission from your Android device.

Location information is used for features such as:

  • Local prayer time calculation.
  • Qibla direction and bearing.
  • Nearby mosque discovery.
  • Reverse geocoding and location display.

Location permission is optional. If you deny location access, features that require location may not be available or may ask you to provide a location through available functionality.

The App may locally cache the most recently available coordinates so that certain prayer calculations can continue to work when the device is offline.

Privacy note: The App does not intentionally perform continuous background location tracking for advertising or general analytics.

5. Quran, Hadith & Islamic Content

Core Quran, Hadith, Dua, Kalima, Asmaul Husna, Islamic names and related resources are primarily provided from local application databases and bundled resources.

These resources are designed to remain available without requiring an internet connection.

The App does not require users to submit personal information in order to read the core Quran, Hadith or Islamic content.

6. Zakat & Financial Information

The Zakat calculator allows users to enter financial values required to estimate their Zakat obligation.

Zakat calculation history is stored locally on the device using the App's local database.

We do not intentionally send your personal Zakat history or financial calculation records to advertising or analytics services.

When live metal prices or currency exchange information is required, the App may communicate with third-party APIs to obtain market data.

Important: Zakat calculations are provided as a utility feature and should not be considered professional financial, tax or religious advice.

7. Advertising & User Consent

Deen Al Islam may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, application interactions and other information permitted by applicable law.

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide users with advertising consent and privacy choices.

Your consent choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized advertising is displayed.

You can manage available privacy choices through the consent mechanisms presented by the App and through applicable Android settings.

8. Firebase Services

The App may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may process application usage and technical information, such as application events and screen interactions, to help improve the App.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used to deliver general notifications and updates to the device.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable privacy practices.

9. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Deen Al Islam may send prayer reminders and general application notifications.

Prayer reminders may use Android's alarm scheduling functionality.

You can disable notifications through the App's settings or your Android device settings.

10. Local Data Storage

Several features of Deen Al Islam are designed to work offline.

Depending on your use of the App, information such as preferences, cached location information, bookmarks, Tasbih state and Zakat history may be stored locally on your device.

Local information generally remains under the control of your device and can be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App, subject to Android system behavior.

11. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide specific functionality.

Examples may include:

  • Google AdMob for advertising.
  • Google Firebase for analytics and messaging.
  • Prayer calculation services where applicable.
  • Geocoding services for location names.
  • Map services for mosque discovery.
  • Market and currency APIs for Zakat-related information.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the App.

Network communications used by the App are intended to use encrypted HTTPS connections where supported by the relevant service.

However, no electronic storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention

Locally stored information generally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.

Data processed by third-party services may be retained according to those providers' respective privacy policies and retention practices.

14. Your Choices

You can control several permissions and application settings, including:

  • Location permission.
  • Notification permission.
  • Prayer alert settings.
  • Advertising consent choices where applicable.
  • Theme and language preferences.

Revoking a permission may prevent certain features from functioning correctly.

15. Children's Privacy

Deen Al Islam is not specifically directed at children under the applicable minimum age for independent consent in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the App's functionality.

16. Third-Party Services

The App may interact with third-party services, websites or applications.

These may include advertising, analytics, messaging, prayer calculation, geocoding, mapping, currency exchange and market-price services.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the applicable policies of those services.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

18. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Deen Al Islam, please contact us.
